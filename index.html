Quote
Make it work, then make it small.

No cloud.

Notes are saved on your phone and nowhere else. There is no server holding a copy.

No account.

No email, no password, no sign-up screen. Open the app and write.

No tracking.

The app collects nothing about you. Nobody reads a word you write, including us.

As simple as a notepad, with a few things paper can't do.

One button

Tap plus, write, save. That is the whole app.

Everything at a glance

Notes sit three across, so a whole screen of them is visible at once.

Hold to read

Press and hold a note to enlarge it. Let go and it drops back.

Swipe to bin

Swipe a note left to delete it. Changed your mind? Undo brings it back.

Colour and star

Seven paper tints to sort by, and a star for the notes that matter.

Flip to dark

One round button inverts the whole app, ink for paper.

Free.

Soon on Google Play for Android phones. Open it, write something down. Nothing to register and nothing to pay.
